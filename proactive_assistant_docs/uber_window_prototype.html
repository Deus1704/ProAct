Research note: Uber’s old embeddable widget was deprecated. The current rider experience uses deep links, and `m.uber.com` responds with `X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN`, which prevents a true iframe embed from another origin.

Recommended demo framing: keep this as a branded Uber handoff panel inside your product. Let users review the suggestion here, then launch Uber in a new tab or mobile deep link for the final native flow.
